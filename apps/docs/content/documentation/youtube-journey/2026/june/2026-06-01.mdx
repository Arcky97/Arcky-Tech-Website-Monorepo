## Pokémon Abyssal Shroud Episode 1

Of course only doing Pokémon Card Product Openings would end up to be really expensive so I decided to give a fan game a try and make a let's play out of it. 

Since I asked for some suggestion in my discord server, the game called Abyssal Shroud came out as the winner for a first new let's play.

Even though the game was pretty new and was not very known (yet), it definitly did not do bad at all. 

I'll just keep saying it, I only just started so I was satisfied with the results I was getting.
