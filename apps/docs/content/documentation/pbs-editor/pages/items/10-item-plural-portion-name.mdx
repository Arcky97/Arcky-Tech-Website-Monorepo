## Plural Portion Name

<InputText label="Plural Portion Name:" value="bottles"/>

The <Or>Plural Portion Name</Or> defines the <Gr>plural form of the portion name</Gr> of the item.
<br/>
When the play has found multiple of a same item, instead of "The player found 5 Repels", it could say "The player found 5 bottles of Repel".
<br/>
---

### Default Value 

<Or>Plural Portion Name</Or> does not have a default value.
